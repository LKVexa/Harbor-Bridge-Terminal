# Licence not declared (MC-074 — BLOCKED on the rights holder)

No licence was supplied with INV-34 and none has been invented. Every production source carries
`SPDX-License-Identifier: NOASSERTION`, `pyproject.toml` carries no licence field, and the SBOM
reports `NOASSERTION`. The rights holder must choose a licence, add `LICENSE`, and replace the
NOASSERTION markers; `tools/ci.py` then needs its SPDX lane changed to require that identifier.
