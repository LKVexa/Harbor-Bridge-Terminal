## Interpretation

- **Implemented** means this standalone archive contains a concrete implementation or directly inspectable evidence for the requirement at the reference-component level.
- **Partial** means meaningful evidence exists, but at least one production-critical portion of the requirement is absent or delegated.
- **Missing** means the standalone archive contains no adequate implementation/evidence for the requirement. These items are summarized as concrete missing components in `AUDIT_REPORT.md`.
