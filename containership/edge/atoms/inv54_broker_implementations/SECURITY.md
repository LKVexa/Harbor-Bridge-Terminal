# Security policy  (component 45)
**Report a vulnerability:** email davidpaulrussell@linearfinance.org with subject `INV-54 SECURITY`. Do not open public issues.
**Triage:** acknowledgement within 3 business days; severity per CVSS 3.1; fix SLAs in `docs/PATCHING_EOL.md`.
**Disclosure:** coordinated; advisory published with the fixed release and CHANGELOG entry.
**Supported versions:** 4.3.x (current), 4.2.x (security fixes until 2027-03-22).
