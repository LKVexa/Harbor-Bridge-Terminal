# INV-63 normative requirements (INV-63-C011)

Generated by `audit.py` from `tools/requirements_src.py`. Each SHALL is verified by the tests listed in
`TRACEABILITY.md`.


## Architecture & Scope

- **INV-63-C001** — The manager SHALL own desired-state storage, diff computation, spread, convergence and bounded rollouts only.
- **INV-63-C002** — The manager SHALL NOT define manifests, run components, sign artifacts or provision hosts.
- **INV-63-C003** — Upstream INV-64, downstream INV-60/INV-66 and peer GAP-08 dependencies SHALL be declared.
- **INV-63-C004** — The journaled desired-state record SHALL be the source of truth; lattice state is observed and corrected.
- **INV-63-C005** — Every node/runtime/network/storage/control-plane assumption SHALL be catalogued and, where feasible, checked at startup.
- **INV-63-C006** — Tenant, environment, site and workload boundaries SHALL be enforced.
- **INV-63-C007** — Mandatory vs optional behaviour SHALL be declared.
- **INV-63-C008** — Non-goals SHALL be declared.
- **INV-63-C009** — An accountable owner, on-call rotation, security contact, release approver and escalation path SHALL be recorded and validated.
- **INV-63-C010** — An approved ADR SHALL record Wadm selection, role, alternatives, boundaries, data ownership, failure model and acceptance criteria.

## Requirements & Semantics

- **INV-63-C011** — Desired-state reconciliation SHALL be specified as testable SHALL requirements (this catalogue).
- **INV-63-C012** — Functional behaviour SHALL be defined per tier: cloud, datacenter, near-edge and far-edge (offline autonomy, constrained limits).
- **INV-63-C013** — Latency, availability, durability, consistency, isolation and determinism SHALL have numeric NFRs verified by tests/benchmarks.
- **INV-63-C014** — Every response SHALL carry one of SUCCESS, PARTIAL, DEGRADED, RETRYABLE, TERMINAL with defined semantics.
- **INV-63-C015** — Lifecycle states and legal transitions SHALL be defined and illegal transitions rejected.
- **INV-63-C016** — Contracts SHALL be semver'd by schema major; minors additive; deprecations dated; the legacy Manager API preserved.
- **INV-63-C017** — Per-tenant instance quotas, request-rate fairness and global capacity ceilings SHALL be enforced.
- **INV-63-C018** — With intermittent/absent connectivity the manager SHALL journal intents, report DEGRADED within the autonomy window, fail RETRYABLE beyond it, and resynchronise on reconnect.
- **INV-63-C019** — Conflicts SHALL resolve in precedence security > residency > consistency > SLO > cost.
- **INV-63-C020** — A traceability matrix requirement->design->implementation->test->evidence->owner SHALL be generated per release.

## Interfaces & Integration

- **INV-63-C021** — Every exposed boundary SHALL be inventoried with contract, authN, authZ, timeout, limits and owner.
- **INV-63-C022** — Every external contract SHALL have a versioned JSON schema validated at the boundary.
- **INV-63-C023** — Every inbound boundary SHALL authenticate callers (signed, expiring, replay-protected tokens).
- **INV-63-C024** — Every operation SHALL require an explicit capability, scoped to the caller's tenant.
- **INV-63-C025** — Timeouts, cancellation, retries (idempotent only), idempotency keys and backpressure SHALL be defined and enforced.
- **INV-63-C026** — Every failure SHALL be a stable code with retryability and machine-readable details.
- **INV-63-C027** — Peers SHALL negotiate the highest common schema major and fail closed when none exists.
- **INV-63-C028** — Payload, depth, concurrency, queue, rate and size limits SHALL be documented and enforced.
- **INV-63-C029** — Reference examples and digest-bound conformance fixtures SHALL ship with the package.
- **INV-63-C030** — Automated tests SHALL prove interoperation with INV-64 (desired input), INV-60 (lattice) and INV-66 (controller).

## Implementation & Configuration

- **INV-63-C031** — Wadm and all runtime dependencies SHALL be pinned by version and digest and approved.
- **INV-63-C032** — Immutable package, mutable config and mutable state SHALL be physically separate.
- **INV-63-C033** — Configuration SHALL be declarative with secure defaults (prod profile by default).
- **INV-63-C034** — Configuration SHALL be validated before activation and fail closed on security-critical errors.
- **INV-63-C035** — Site/environment overlays SHALL change behaviour without rebuilding the package.
- **INV-63-C036** — Each activation SHALL record version, digest, author, time, reason and previous digest.
- **INV-63-C037** — Configuration activation SHALL be atomic (all-or-nothing).
- **INV-63-C038** — Failed rollouts SHALL auto-rollback; operators SHALL be able to roll back workloads and configuration.
- **INV-63-C039** — Secrets SHALL appear only as refs (env:/file:) and SHALL be redacted from logs, traces and errors.
- **INV-63-C040** — Bootstrap from an empty node SHALL be deterministic and gated by preflight.

## Security, Trust & Isolation

- **INV-63-C041** — A STRIDE threat model SHALL cover tenants, workloads, inputs, supply chain, control plane and insiders, each threat mapped to controls and tests.
- **INV-63-C042** — Each role SHALL hold the minimum capability set; platform principals SHALL NOT mutate tenant workloads.
- **INV-63-C043** — The service SHALL use only injected handles (adapter, journal dir); no ambient FS/network/device/secret authority.
- **INV-63-C044** — Nodes, peers, artifacts, providers and controllers SHALL be authenticated before trust.
- **INV-63-C045** — Artifacts SHALL be verified by digest, Ed25519 signature from a trusted key id, approved version and revocation list before deploy or rollout.
- **INV-63-C046** — Tenant state, identifiers, quotas and placement SHALL be isolated.
- **INV-63-C047** — Sensitive state SHALL be encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) with key ids and rotation; transit SHALL use the transport's TLS/mTLS.
- **INV-63-C048** — When identity, key, verifier or time services are unavailable the manager SHALL fail closed.
- **INV-63-C049** — Security-sensitive operations SHALL emit hash-chained, fsync'd audit records; tampering SHALL be detected at load.
- **INV-63-C050** — Adversarial tests SHALL cover escalation, injection, replay, spoofing, escape and exhaustion.

## Resilience & Failure Handling

- **INV-63-C051** — Component/process/node/site/network/provider/dependency/control-plane failures SHALL be enumerated with detection and response.
- **INV-63-C052** — Health and stall thresholds SHALL be defined and detected automatically.
- **INV-63-C053** — Retries SHALL be bounded, jittered and limited to idempotent operations and retryable errors.
- **INV-63-C054** — Admission control, priority shedding and circuit breaking SHALL prevent cascades.
- **INV-63-C055** — Failover SHALL NOT violate isolation, residency or consistency (fenced leader; residency-constrained placement).
- **INV-63-C056** — Non-critical dependency loss SHALL degrade (not fail) the service.
- **INV-63-C057** — State SHALL be crash-consistent; restart SHALL replay the journal and truncate only torn tails.
- **INV-63-C058** — Stale controllers SHALL be fenced by epoch; concurrent writers SHALL be detected.
- **INV-63-C059** — Operators SHALL be able to freeze, quarantine (workload/tenant/host) and emergency-disable automation.
- **INV-63-C060** — Fault-injection tests SHALL prove recovery objectives.

## Performance & Resource Efficiency

- **INV-63-C061** — Reproducible baselines SHALL be recorded for latency, throughput, startup, memory and storage.
- **INV-63-C062** — p50/p95/p99/max thresholds SHALL be defined.
- **INV-63-C063** — Steady, burst, overload, scale-out, scale-in and recovery SHALL be measured.
- **INV-63-C064** — Per-tenant and per-workload overhead SHALL be measured.
- **INV-63-C065** — Avoidable serialization, copies and duplicated state SHALL be identified.
- **INV-63-C066** — Safe optimizations SHALL be applied with semantic-equivalence proof.
- **INV-63-C067** — Memory, queues, buffers, concurrency and fan-out SHALL be bounded.
- **INV-63-C068** — Power and thermal impact SHALL be measured on constrained edge nodes.
- **INV-63-C069** — A capacity model and saturation signals SHALL predict resource needs.
- **INV-63-C070** — Releases SHALL be blocked when performance regresses beyond approved thresholds.

## Observability & Explainability

- **INV-63-C071** — Health, readiness, version, config digest, dependency status and capabilities SHALL be exposed.
- **INV-63-C072** — RED/USE metrics SHALL be emitted in Prometheus format.
- **INV-63-C073** — Logs SHALL be structured with node, tenant, workload, component, operation and trace ids.
- **INV-63-C074** — W3C trace context SHALL propagate across request, decision and adapter boundaries.
- **INV-63-C075** — Diagnostics SHALL be cardinality-bounded, redacted and tenant-pseudonymised when exported.
- **INV-63-C076** — Every automated decision SHALL record its reason, inputs, policies and constraints.
- **INV-63-C077** — An explain view SHALL link decisions to inputs, policies, topology and constraints.
- **INV-63-C078** — Decisions SHALL carry release lineage and a topology digest.
- **INV-63-C079** — Telemetry retention, sampling, privacy and export SHALL be defined.
- **INV-63-C080** — Dashboards and alerts SHALL distinguish load, degradation, policy rejection, dependency failure, security events and stalls.

## Testing & Certification

- **INV-63-C081** — Unit tests SHALL cover core logic.
- **INV-63-C082** — Every public interface SHALL have contract tests.
- **INV-63-C083** — Integration tests SHALL exercise every adjacent layer and execution tier.
- **INV-63-C084** — Compatibility SHALL be tested across CPU architectures, runtimes, providers and protocol versions.
- **INV-63-C085** — Parsers, schemas and untrusted inputs SHALL be fuzzed.
- **INV-63-C086** — Concurrency and race tests SHALL cover shared/distributed state.
- **INV-63-C087** — Security tests SHALL derive directly from the threat model.
- **INV-63-C088** — Benchmark, soak, burst and fleet-scale tests SHALL exist.
- **INV-63-C089** — Disaster, partition, reconnect and degraded-control-plane tests SHALL exist.
- **INV-63-C090** — Machine-readable, signed acceptance evidence SHALL be required before production certification.

## Operations, Release & Governance

- **INV-63-C091** — Production SLOs, error budgets and support commitments SHALL be defined.
- **INV-63-C092** — Canary, staged rollout, rollback and emergency-disable procedures SHALL be defined and exercised.
- **INV-63-C093** — A supported-version compatibility matrix SHALL be maintained.
- **INV-63-C094** — Patching, vulnerability response and EOL SLAs SHALL be defined.
- **INV-63-C095** — Backup, restore, migration and reconstruction procedures SHALL exist and be tested.
- **INV-63-C096** — Day-0/1/2 runbooks SHALL exist.
- **INV-63-C097** — Incident severity, paging, escalation, containment and recovery SHALL be defined.
- **INV-63-C098** — Access, policy, dependency, configuration and architecture reviews SHALL recur on a schedule enforced by the gate.
- **INV-63-C099** — Exceptions, waivers, debt and deprecations SHALL be tracked with owners and expiry, and expired waivers SHALL block release.
- **INV-63-C100** — A single signed production exit gate SHALL consume all evidence bound to the exact artifact/config/dependencies.
